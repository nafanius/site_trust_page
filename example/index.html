Common Questions

How long does the green card process take?＋
Processing times vary by category. Family-based petitions can take 12–30 months. Employment-based categories range from 6 months (premium processing) to several years depending on priority date.
Do you offer free consultations?＋
Yes. We provide a complimentary 30-minute case assessment for all new clients. You can book directly through our website or by calling our office.
Can you help if my application was previously refused?＋
Absolutely. We regularly succeed with cases that were previously denied. We conduct a full review and develop a new strategy tailored to your situation.
