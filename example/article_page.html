Understanding the EB-5 Investor Visa in 2026

The EB-5 Immigrant Investor Program remains one of the most direct pathways to U.S. permanent residence for foreign investors. In 2026, the program continues to evolve with updated investment thresholds and stricter regional center oversight.

The minimum investment amount is currently $800,000 for projects located in targeted employment areas (TEAs) and $1,050,000 for standard investments. Investors must also demonstrate that their capital will create at least 10 full-time jobs for U.S. workers.

Recent regulatory changes have introduced new priority date rules and enhanced scrutiny on source of funds documentation. Regional centers must now comply with stricter reporting requirements to maintain designation.

Applicants should carefully evaluate project viability, job creation projections, and the track record of the regional center before committing funds. Many investors now work with experienced immigration counsel throughout the entire process.

Processing times for EB-5 petitions currently range from 12 to 30 months depending on the category and whether premium processing is available. Investors are encouraged to begin the process early to account for potential delays.
