Company Formation, Administration & Accounting in Poland

One person who knows your business.

Every Polish company has tasks that someone has to take care of: onboard a new employee, renew documents, collect a certificate from ZUS, respond to the accountant, or visit a government office. TrustInPoland takes care of all of this: you pay a fixed fee and get an administrative assistant without having to hire an in-house employee.

We set up and support companies across a wide range of industries — from IT and retail to beauty salons and agencies. Construction companies sending employees to Germany, Belgium, France and Lithuania can also benefit from a dedicated service package: a per-employee subscription and one-off business travel services.

New applications, document amendments, renewals and correspondence with government offices are all included — there is no separate charge for each action. Only government fees and services outside the subscription, such as company registration and legal services, are charged separately.
